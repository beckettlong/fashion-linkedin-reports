# Spec: Collections & Collaborations (Wednesday post)

This file controls exactly what the weekly drops report pulls. Edit it freely —
the script reads it at runtime and passes it to Claude as the editorial brief.
No code changes needed. Everything below is instructions to the researcher.

## What counts as an item

Include a drop if it is a **collaboration, capsule collection, or special/limited
release** in fashion, footwear, apparel, or accessories that was **announced or
released** within the report window.

Include:
- Brand × brand collaborations (e.g. Birkenstock 1774 × Repetto)
- Brand × celebrity / designer / artist capsules (e.g. Gap × Hailey Bieber)
- Mass-retail designer partnerships (e.g. Rosie Assoulin × Target)
- Independent / emerging label seasonal collections with a clear point of view
- Sport & performance collabs tied to a moment (World Cup, Olympics, league tie-ins)
- Luxury house crossovers into streetwear/footwear/eyewear

Exclude:
- Routine seasonal restocks or core-line color updates with no collaboration angle
- Pure marketing campaigns with no product attached
- Resale/secondary-market news
- Anything you cannot source to a named publication or official brand announcement

## How many

Aim for **4–8 items**. Prioritize by:
1. Size of the brands involved
2. Novelty of the pairing (unexpected > predictable)
3. Cultural relevance of the moment it ties into
4. Mix — try to end up with a spread across categories rather than five sneaker
   collabs. A good week has some combination of: one big celebrity capsule, one
   mass-retail designer partnership, one footwear collab, one indie/emerging
   label, one luxury or performance crossover.

## What to include per item

- Both/all brands, spelled and capitalized as the brand styles itself
  (STORY mfg., adidas, SKIMS — match their own casing)
- The collection or product name, in quotes if it has one
- Release or announcement date
- Price or price range, if reported
- Where it's available (online, select stores, region-exclusive)
- Design details: silhouettes, materials, inspiration, notable design features
- Named people involved (designers, creative directors, the celebrity partner)

## Tone

Neutral trade-press. Factual and specific. No hype adjectives ("insane," "must-
have," "fire"), no second-person address, no emoji inside item bodies. Write the
way Business of Fashion or Drapers would write a one-paragraph news brief.

## Preferred sources

Lead with: Business of Fashion, WWD, FashionNetwork, Drapers, SGB Media,
Hypebeast, Highsnobiety, FashionUnited, official brand newsrooms and press releases.

## Hard rules

- Never invent a brand, person, date, price, or product detail. If you cannot
  verify it via search, leave it out.
- If you find fewer than 4 verifiable items, report fewer — do not pad.
- Every item must trace to a real URL that you list in the SOURCES section.
